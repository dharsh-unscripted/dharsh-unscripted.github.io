Sprouts for Learning

April 2025

Students in rural areas often lack access to books, which leads to lower literacy rates. At Sprouts for Learning, we work with student writers and illustrators to create books to combat this issue. These stories are not just stories; they are gateways into cultures (folktales) or talk about important issues such as child marriage or kindness. Our stories will help underprivileged students gain access to books, provide student writers and illustrators with the opportunity to harness their passion and positively impact their community, and expose students to diverse cultures and societal issues. Currently, we support over 5,000 students in 10 countries by providing them with books, worksheets, and other learning resources/opportunities.

One of the core goals of Sprouts for Learning is to promote multicultural education. By partnering with the Learn with Fun organization in Pakistan, we have facilitated a letter exchange between 300 students in Korea and 400 in Pakistan. The students made cultural crafts such as ddakji and bookmarks with Korea’s traditional clothes, hanbok. It was a truly memorable experience for students.

Sprouts for Learning’s worksheets are used by students around the world. These worksheets are often aimed at elementary students, thus they include a variety of fun and interesting activities to learn English. They also support educators in creating a solid base in terms of vocabulary, grammar, and reading for their students.
