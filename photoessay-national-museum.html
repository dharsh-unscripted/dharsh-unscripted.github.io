Trip to the National Museum of Korea - Joseon Era

August 2025

This past weekend, I had the opportunity to visit the National Museum of Korea’s special exhibit on Art of Early Joseon: Masterpieces from the 15th and 16th Century. This exhibit took me to the advent of the Joseon Dynasty in 1392 - a period marked with a surge of national pride, which led to a distinctive new era in Korean art. Material, cultural, and technological achievements from previous eras were coalesced to reflect “the ideas and aesthetic aspirations of the new Confucian State,” creating completely new types of art that the nation had never seen before.
