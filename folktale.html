Sprouts for Learning: Folktales Project

Sprouts for Learning has shifted from just being an organization that helps teach students English and improve literacy, to also empowering students to become authors and illustrators while deepening their understanding of Asian culture and history. Through the creation of these books, students develop essential reading, writing, research, and artistic skills while sharing meaningful cultural stories with under-served communities.

Under-served students in nations such as Korea, Japan, China, Hongkong, and America receive these books and get the chance to not only improve their literacy through reading, but also to grow as a global citizen as they are exposed to a myriad of cultures and histories.
