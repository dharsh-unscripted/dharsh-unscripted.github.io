Little Bit About Dharshwana

Dharshwana Muralidharan is a senior at Gyeonggi Suwon International School (GSIS). Throughout her high school career, she has been interested in a myriad of subjects. However, one of the subjects she finds the most interesting is the field of Asian studies. Growing up at the crossroads of two Asian cultures ( Korean and Indian ), she is deeply interested in the exploration of Asian history, culture, and the contemporary dynamics of Asia. This interest is reflected in not only her research work, which has been recognized by international organizations such as the International Political Science Association (IPSA) and the Harvard International Review (HIR), but also is manifest in her theatre and volunteer work. Dharshwana is a theatre enthusiast, and has written and directed two plays focused on modern-Korean history. She has combined her interest in theatre with deep research into Korea’s modern history, such as the colonial period and the Gwangju Uprising, in order to produce historically accurate plays. Dharshwana believes that theatre is a medium for bringing awareness to historical issues to the younger generation so that past mistakes from history are not repeated. Finally, Dharshwana is a strong advocate for education. She has founded and led Sprouts for Learning which has mobilized volunteer groups within Korea and provided English worksheets to students. While Sprouts for Learning initially began as only an organization that provided English education, Dharshwana saw how culture integrated into education to promote literary as well as globally-minded citizens. It was at this point, Sprouts for Learning pivoted and started to not only provide students with materials to learn English, but worked with student writers and illustrators to create and distribute 200+ books containing folktales for Asian and European countries, with the goal of not only helping students practice their english through reading, but also to provide them with the opportunity to learn about new cultures and countries.
