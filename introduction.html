Introduction

Hi! My name is Dharshwana. I’m currently a junior at Gyeonggi Suwon International School! Welcome to my blog! You’ll find a wide variety of articles on my opinions and my experiences! I like to read works by James Baldwin, John Steinbeck, Virginia Woolf, and, of course, Jane Austen in my free time. I’m also a theatre enthusiast, but you’ll find me more down the stage blocking a scene or profusely making notes about how to make a monologue better than on the stage! I love learning about obscure social issues in the East Asia region. I’m a philosophy junkie ( particularly Michel Foucault). In my free time, I like to binge Netflix shows and travel. I hope you enjoy my blog!
