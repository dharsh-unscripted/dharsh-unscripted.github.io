TEDx Event at Gyeonggi Suwon International School (GSIS)

January 2025

TEDx Youth events are a wonderful way to give the younger generation a platform to showcase their thoughts, values, beliefs, and jaw-dropping ideas for the future. I had the privilege of being able to organize such an event. With students from 5+ international schools in South Korea, TEDx Gyeonggi Suwon International School was a success in helping students build confidence and share their wonderful ideas with the world. The theme for the event was “GET THE FACTS STRAIGHT.” Our modern society is no stranger to polarization, which is often perpetuated by the rampage of fake/biased news consumed by citizens. At this event, we challenged speakers to dig deeper into their chosen topic to find obscure truths and not to be afraid to explore diverse ideas and perspectives.
